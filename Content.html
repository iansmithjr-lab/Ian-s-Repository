Software

PsychoPy provides help to psychology by letting researchers build experiments, present stimuli, and collect data. It gives scientists tools for visual and auditory control, precise timing and measurements, and online or lab testing. PsyToolkit provides a free, web-based platform used to program, run, and analyze online cognitive-psychological experiments, behavioral research studies, and personality surveys. It offers built-in libraries of peer-reviewed questionnaires and precise reaction-time tests, allowing researchers and students to collect data globally via browser links.
